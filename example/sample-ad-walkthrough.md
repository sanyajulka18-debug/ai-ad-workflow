# Sample ad walkthrough: BrewBright cold coffee

One sample ad taken through every template, from a one-line brief to the final check.
**BrewBright is a made-up product** used only to show the process.

**Brief:** "20-second street-interview ad for BrewBright ready-to-drink cold coffee. Young office crowd. Offer: buy 2, get 1 free."

---

## 1. Brand sheet (filled)
- **Product:** BrewBright Cold Coffee, 250 ml can
- **Look:** matte black can, orange "BrewBright" logo across the middle, white text "Cold Coffee · 250 ml" below
- **Product moment:** can opened with a click, poured over ice into a clear glass
- **CAN claim:** "Ready to drink", "Made with real coffee", "No added sugar option"
- **AVOID:** any health or energy claim ("boosts energy", "healthy")
- **Pronunciation:** BrewBright = "BROO-bright" (one word, not "Brew Bright")
- **CTA wording:** "Buy 2, get 1 free — tap Shop Now"

## 2. Reference breakdown (summary)
- Format: street interview, handheld, natural daylight, 18 s, 11 shots (about 1.6 s each)
- Hook: host shoves the mic in frame and asks a question in the first second
- Captions: bold, word by word, centre of frame
- **Copy into our ad:** mic in frame from frame 1; quick reaction shots; product reveal at around 70%

## 3. Script

```
[HOOK 1]
HOST: What's in your bag right now that gets you through 3 p.m.?

[HOOK 2]
HOST: Be honest — how many coffees have you had today?

[HOOK 3]
HOST: I'll give you a free coffee if you can guess what's in my hand.

[BODY]
WOMAN: Honestly? Office coffee. And it's terrible.
HOST: Ever tried a cold one in a can?
WOMAN: In a can? No way that's good.
HOST: Try it.
(Visual: she sips. Pause. Eyebrows up.)
WOMAN: Okay… that's actually really good.

[CTA]
HOST: BrewBright. Real coffee, ready to drink. Buy 2, get 1 free — tap Shop Now.
(On screen: Buy 2, get 1 free)
```

## 4. Clip plan + one prompt

| Clip | Lines | Length |
|---|---|---|
| H1 / H2 / H3 | one hook each | 3–4 s |
| B1 | Woman: office coffee → "No way that's good" | 7 s |
| B2 | "Try it" → sip → "actually really good" | 6 s |
| CTA | Host to camera + product | 5 s |

**Prompt for B2:**
```
CLIP: B2 · 6 seconds · vertical 9:16
SCENE: busy office-district pavement, late afternoon, glass buildings behind
PEOPLE: HOST — woman, mid-20s, denim jacket, white tee, holding a black handheld mic.
        WOMAN — late 20s, office wear, navy blazer, hair tied back, tote bag on shoulder.
PRODUCT: BrewBright can — matte black can, orange "BrewBright" logo, white text "Cold Coffee · 250 ml"
CAMERA: handheld medium two-shot, small push-in on the sip
LIGHT & COLOUR: warm late-afternoon daylight, natural colour
ACTION: host hands her the open can; she sips; pauses; eyebrows go up; she smiles
DIALOGUE (exact words, nothing added):
HOST (warm, playful, Indian-English accent): "Try it."
WOMAN (surprised, light laugh, Indian-English accent): "Okay… that's actually really good."
SOUND: street traffic, footsteps, light crowd chatter; no music
AVOID: extra words, text on screen, logo changes, can turning a different colour
```
